## Team Purpose

### Our Objective

The main goal of our team is to design, validate, and present a market-ready solution that addresses the unemployment and skills-access problem in underserved township communities. Specifically, we aim to develop a hyperlocal mobile platform that connects skilled informal workers with households that need small tasks completed (the TradeLoop concept) and to demonstrate through research and evidence that this is a real, validated opportunity worth pursuing.

### Our Vision Statement

> *"Our vision is a community where no skilled person goes unnoticed and no household waits too long for help. **TradeLoop** succeeds when daily micro-work becomes a consistent, trusted, and dignified income stream for workers in **Khayelitsha** and communities like it across South Africa."*

## Team Values and Principles

### Core Values

These are the values we agreed on as a group that will guide how we work together throughout this project:

- **Collaboration:** We treat every idea as a team contribution. Decisions are made together, and no one carries the whole load.
- **Respect:** We listen to each other, honour different perspectives, and never dismiss a contribution without reason.
- **Transparency:** We keep each other updated on progress and challenges. No one should be surprised by a missed deadline or a quality issue.
- **Accountability:** We each own our role and take responsibility for our deliverables. If something goes wrong, we fix it together rather than deflecting blame.
- **Continuous improvement:** After each submission or sprint, we reflect on what worked and what we need to do better next time.

### Agile Key Principles We Follow

We have grounded our teamwork in the following Agile principles, which we believe are directly applicable to a student project environment:

- **Deliver working value early:** Our survey was designed and executed in 3 days so we could have real evidence before writing conclusions.
- **Welcome changing requirements:** As we gathered data, we adjusted our problem framing and solution without discarding prior work.
- **Build around motivated individuals:** Each role was assigned based on genuine strengths, not just filling slots.
- **Regular reflection and adaptation:** After each deliverable phase, we do a brief retrospective to improve as a team.
- **Simplicity:** We focus on what is needed for each deliverable and do not overcompensate our outputs.

### Meeting Frequency and Structure

- **Sprint Planning Meeting:** Once per deliverable cycle, before work begins.
  - Agenda: divide tasks, agree on deadlines, and clarify requirements.
- **Check-in / Stand-up:** Twice per week via WhatsApp voice note or short video call.
  - Each member answers: What did I finish? What am I working on? Is anything blocking me?
- **Review Meeting:** One day before each submission.
  - Agenda: review the full document together, give feedback, and finalise the version to submit.
- **Retrospective:** After each submission, we discuss what went well and what we will change for the next sprint.
- If a member cannot attend a meeting, they must send a written update to the WhatsApp group at least 1 hour before the meeting starts.

## Goals and Milestones

### SMART Goals

**Goal 1: Community Problem Validation**
To conduct a structured community survey with a minimum of 20 respondents from Khayelitsha Site C and present validated evidence of the problem within 3 days of the survey launch.
*Status: Completed. 21 respondents, 100% problem confirmation.*

**Goal 2: Business Idea Development**
To develop a clearly defined business concept (TradeLoop) with a named target market, revenue model, and value proposition.
*Status: Completed.*

**Goal 3: Prototype / MVP Design**
To produce a functional wireframe or clickable prototype of the TradeLoop app by the date set for Deliverable 2, demonstrating the core user flow for both consumers and skilled workers.

**Goal 4: Presentation Delivery**
To deliver a confident, well-structured group presentation of the TradeLoop project covering problem, evidence, solution, and business model within the allocated time slot, with all members contributing.

**Goal 5: Academic Quality**
To achieve a minimum group average of 70% across all deliverables by maintaining consistent referencing, clear writing, and evidence-based argumentation throughout all submissions.
